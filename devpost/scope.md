# LibreY

LibreY is a lively, social-style feed where spare moments become a fun way to learn from real public-domain books.

## The Unique Kernel
It feels like the entertaining feed people already want to keep scrolling, but gives them something worthwhile to learn. Posts are grounded in real book passages and lead straight to the source, so the fun does not come at the cost of trust or substance.

## Who It's For
People who enjoy social feeds but wish that a few minutes of scrolling left them with something interesting to know.

## The Core Loop
Choose or skip topic preferences, open the feed, and find a short, natural and entertaining post from a book or subject that catches your interest. For You gradually learns from dwell time and clicks, testing new topics and reducing ones the reader consistently passes over. A reader can follow a book from a post’s full view and see followed books’ posts in Following. Tapping through opens the exact passage in the book. The post’s AI control offers more context and follow-up questions, with broader-knowledge answers identified as such.

## Inspiration & Identity
Familiar and easy to browse like mainstream social feeds, but minimal, light, and carefully spaced. Most posts should feel fun and conversational; only a few should be formal articles. Post details can show whether a post was AI-generated, while a brief explanation at the start sets that expectation without making AI labels dominate the feed.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
“I don't want to doomscroll everytime without gaining almost anything in knowledge.” LibreY should make browsing feel enjoyable while offering real learning along the way.

## What "Working" Looks Like
In a short demo, someone opens a lively feed, enjoys a few concise posts that teach them something, and taps one to see the real book passage it came from. The feed feels natural and fun, and the source makes the learning verifiable.

## The POC Boundary
Build a focused feed-to-source experience: optional topic onboarding; a small, curated selection of real public-domain books from more than one area; and a mostly text-led feed of natural, varied posts based on those books. Include the six proposed post kinds, some image posts, a few longer articles, and interactive poll/quiz/debate choices. For You starts from reader interests and demonstrates gradual adjustment from dwell and clicks. Readers can follow books from full-post views and see those books in Following. Each post offers AI context and typed follow-up questions, and the reader view opens the cited chapter and passage. Set an expectation up front that some posts may be AI-generated, with provenance in post details. Keep the visual experience minimal and comfortable to browse.

## Later
- Let readers submit academic posts and have them reviewed.
- Expand personalization beyond the basic topic and dwell/click feedback loop, including more signals and detailed ranking rules.
- Add further community and social interactions, more post formats, and a larger library.
- Expand the use of illustrations and imagery from suitable open sources or generated for the posts.

## Explicitly Cut
- Rooms and chat are excluded from LibreY.
